# Smoke corpus provenance

The twelve numbered text documents were authored specifically for this repository during its initialization. They contain fictional descriptions, repeated prose, and mathematical notation. They were not downloaded or extracted from model outputs presented as real educational material. Their purpose is software testing; repetition and shared wording make them unsuitable for measuring language generalization. They may be used for this project's tokenizer and training smoke tests.

These twelve original fixture documents are dedicated under CC0-1.0; see LICENSE in this directory. This choice does not apply to future external corpora or model weights.
