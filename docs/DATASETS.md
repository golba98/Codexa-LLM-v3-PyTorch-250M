### Approved general-English criteria and historical proposal

The following criteria were proposed after the root-cause investigation and have now been explicitly approved. The original 20-case trial below remains historical diagnostic evidence. The intended model remains the existing 248,687,488-parameter decoder, with no tokenizer, architecture or checkpoint changes.

The previous implementation required every quality dimension to avoid both `defect` and `uncertain`; a small local failure could therefore nullify an otherwise useful complete article. That combines encyclopedic completeness, factual verification and general-language utility. The approved revision keeps the diagnostic dimensions and numerical gates, but separates **defect existence, its effect on the surviving account, and whole-document usefulness**.

| Approved band | Decision | Operational criterion |
|---|---|---|
| U0 | Usable | Coherent, independently meaningful English; harmless formatting, ordinary genre variation or peripheral uncertainty does not defeat it. |
| U1 | Usable, annotate defects | Real local errors/omissions exist, but a reader can follow the predominant surviving account without reconstructing its core entities or relations. The defect is retained in evidence; no text is repaired. |
| R2 | Reject | Substantial semantic damage: recurring missing core subjects/values/relations, predominantly garbled or scaffold-only text, or a materially false central account. Explain what core meaning becomes unavailable or unreliable. |
| H | Hold | A specific unresolved issue materially affects the core account or admissibility. Mere inability to verify an incidental fact is insufficient. |
| X | Outside scope | Navigation/disambiguation, dictionary-only or discussion pages and dedicated mathematics/code material do not enter this article-priority target. Scope is distinct from corruption. |
| C | Critical exclusion | Existing observed protected-evaluation leakage or prohibited sensitive identifiers. This retains the existing critical definition rather than silently classifying all semantic damage as a critical privacy/evaluation event. |

Single typos, incidental missing dates, incomplete auxiliary tables and absent illustrations do not automatically fail a document. Missing media reject only when the surviving content cannot stand independently or is principally scaffolding. A qualified hypothesis is not an assertion of established fact; unsupported is not proved false. Conversely, repeated broken references, materially false central accounts and harmful false instructions remain rejection grounds. Classroom pedagogy and perfect reference reproduction are not requirements for base pretraining. Lists and chronologies can be informative English and are not inherently corrupted.

**Numerical thresholds are unchanged:** at least 90/100 usable documents and 270/300 usable windows; at most two severe documents and 15 severe windows; zero critical findings; two independent reviewers. The unchanged severe definition is substantial destruction of meaning, completeness or reliability. When applying a fixed preserved panel, U0/U1 count usable; R2/H/X/C do not. All 100 identities stay in the denominator; scope losses are separately reported, never silently deleted or replaced. This fixed-denominator scope treatment is included in the explicit authorization. The original source rights, attribution, protected-evaluation, review exclusion, duplicate, split, compute and explicit training-authorization gates remain mandatory.

### Rubric diagnosis and limits

Across the original 100 document ratings, nonexclusive combined flags were 19 completeness, 28 correctness (24 defect, four uncertain), five coherence, seven education, two notation and one visual issue; contamination passed all 100. Thirty-eight documents were rejected by both reviewers and eight by only one. The conservative combination contributes some additional failures, but it does not alone explain why each reviewer accepted fewer than 60 articles.

Substantial missing terms and dates (D034,D085), lost core reference content (D047), and an unexplained intrusive sentence (D026) are appropriate concerns. However, the all-checks-pass rule also makes an incidental missing event year (D033) or a recoverable track/side typo (D060) reject an entire otherwise coherent article. D045 has a genuinely missing figure, but useful explanatory prose is independent of it; a figure essential to one numerical claim is not necessarily essential to the whole document. D029 explicitly frames archaeology as unresolved hypotheses, while the review rationale demands establishment of the inference. D006's release-year challenge is unresolved from the preserved local evidence. D090's missing All-Pro years are real, but joining a team and first playing games in different years is not an established contradiction. These distinguish pretraining utility, strict reference completeness, factual uncertainty and reviewer overinterpretation. The original 54% gate acceptance must not be presented as meaning 46% of the text is useless English.

The source-level distinction is firm only up to the preserved provider field: the omissions/wording predate Codexa's loader and BPE. The original MediaWiki wikitext/HTML was not retained. Template/rendering-sensitive omissions are a plausible upstream mechanism, but this no-download investigation cannot prove which provider step caused them or whether every factual problem was already authored on Wikipedia. Existing external fact-check links are preserved review evidence, not fresh verification in this diagnosis.

The numerical gates remain unchanged (90/100 documents, 270/300 windows, severe ceilings 2/15, zero critical findings). Any proposed clarification of minor-versus-substantive defects, essential visuals, uncertain hypotheses or whole-document usability should be explicitly approved and consistently adjudicated before changing ratings. That diagnosis did not change acceptance rates or approve the source; the subsequent authorized full reevaluation is recorded above. Detailed per-document metadata, comparisons, original reasons, case annotations and input hashes are under `.agents/scratch/wikimedia-diagnosis/`.

## M3 production workflow

`data curate --config CONFIG --output DIR [--resume]` streams UTF-8 JSONL, bounded text paragraphs or optional Parquet batches into SQLite. Text paragraphs retain their source-file split group. Each source needs name, snapshot, license, attribution and paths. A row may provide a split/group identity; declared validation/test sources are processed before training. `configs/data/example.yaml` is a template requiring reviewed local paths.

SQLite stores text, identities, quality decisions, deduplication indexes and committed source cursors. It is the durable spool as well as the index, avoiding a second text copy and a cross-file commit protocol. JSONL/text restart at committed byte offsets; Parquet skips completed row groups and replays at most its current group. Source/configuration hashes must match. Invalid UTF-8/JSON, missing/short/oversize text, NUL/replacement corruption and extreme repetition are counted separately. Text is preserved; normalized whitespace is used only for duplicate identity. Maximum document size defaults to 1 MiB; oversized Parquet row groups require repartitioning. These limits and filtering counts must be reviewed for the intended corpus.

The Python `curate` interface accepts explicit `commit_every`, `cache_mib` and `wal` keyword arguments for preparation I/O tuning. Defaults remain 100 documents, 8 MiB and rollback journaling; these options do not change filtering, split policy or input identity. Larger transactions increase the maximum work replayed after interruption. Recovery may use different I/O settings while preserving committed cursors and retained records. Optional WAL is checkpointed and returned to rollback journaling before publishing the immutable database checksum. The authorized 500M preparation uses 1,000-document transactions, a 256 MiB SQLite page cache and WAL during ingestion.

Optional `protected_cache_entries` bounds an exact in-memory set of protected span identities. Its default is zero, retaining disk-backed lookups. If the complete set exceeds the entry cap, curation discards the cache and uses SQLite; it never uses a truncated set to make filtering decisions. The 500M preparation permits up to two million entries. Tests cover both complete caching and fallback against the same holdout-overlap cases.

Deduplication bands are indexed by both band hash and document identity, so removing a contaminated document does not scan the entire bands table. The document index is added to unfinished databases during preparation or recovery. Completed curated databases remain byte-identical, including older databases without this index.

Exact duplicates are global. Long documents (at least 128 lexical/operator tokens) use 64 deterministic MinHash projections in 16 bands; candidates require verified five-token-shingle Jaccard similarity ≥0.85. One representative is retained. Short mathematics retains exact checking only. This approximate, representative-based detector can miss matches and transitive chains; it is not a completeness guarantee. Protected evaluation text must be supplied as declared holdout sources. A second disk-backed pass removes training documents sharing any 13-token span with retained holdouts. Shorter embedded questions and semantic equivalence remain limitations; production review must address them before the pilot.

Default hash splits are 99/0.5/0.5 percent, grouped by document or supplied parent identity. Declared upstream splits are preserved. The curated immutable database checksum binds all retained split identities. A changed input requires a new dataset; resume cannot silently add new files. Empty splits fail instead of being silently rebalanced.

`tokenizer train --dataset DIR --vocab-size 16384 --output FILE` fits a byte BPE candidate from training only, with an explicit 32 MiB fitting sample budget. Iterator ingestion does not imply constant-memory native BPE statistics; measure fitting memory before increasing the budget. Initial byte alphabet and PAD/UNK/BOS/EOS IDs 0/1/2/3 are preserved. No pretrained tokenizer is imported. Literal reserved-token strings retain legacy special-token semantics.

`tokenizer evaluate --dataset DIR --tokenizer FILE` measures validation round trips, tokens per character/document and fixed arithmetic/Unicode expression fragmentation. `tokenizer freeze` creates a non-overwriting candidate directory, tokenizer and checksummed identity/provenance. Production acceptance is a separate decision. Modifying a frozen tokenizer invalidates preparation; training additionally checks tokenized-manifest fingerprints.

`data prepare --dataset DIR --tokenizer FILE --output NEW_DIR [--resume]` writes little-endian uint16 shards (target 128 MiB), per-document offset indexes and checksum manifests. BOS/document/EOS boundaries are explicit; attention can cross documents. The final incomplete sequence is unused. Resume verifies committed shards and restarts at the first uncommitted document; completed documents are not retokenized. A final manifest is published atomically. Do not run competing writers against one output directory.

`data verify --data DIR` checks schema, sizes and checksums. The training reader maps at most four shards at once, assembles sequence-plus-lookahead windows across shard boundaries and shifts exactly once. ## Bounded pinned snapshots

`codexa data sample --config CONFIG --output NEW_DIR --max-bytes BYTES` additionally supports explicitly reviewed Parquet snapshots, without adding a dataset-library dependency. Each source declares name, immutable URL, SHA256, snapshot, license, attribution and selected `text_bytes`. Both `text` and `url` columns are required. The command downloads each file once, refuses an advertised size above the remaining budget, and verifies its hash before reading. Interrupted downloads are retained for inspection; automatic retry/resume is not provided. The byte limit covers downloaded response bodies, not HTTP headers. Never restart into an existing output directory.

Seeded bottom-hash selection scans each snapshot rather than selecting a file prefix, with per-source text-byte limits. Original URLs remain in local JSONL; canonical host/path identities group source versions before splitting. Groups conservatively omit URL queries and fragments. Existing exact/approximate deduplication and protected 13-token-span checks still apply. They do not prove absence of paraphrased or embedded short benchmark questions.

Tokenizer training optionally accepts `--config CONFIG` containing `source_bytes: {SOURCE_NAME: FITTING_BYTES}`. This selects fitting documents from training only, ordered by document hash, under both per-source limits and `--max-bytes`. Without this option the legacy fitting sequence is preserved. `reject_reserved_tokens: true` in curation rejects literal reserved-token spellings; special-token semantics are unchanged. Verification additionally checks every token range, increasing document offsets and BOS/EOS boundary placement even when file checksums match.
