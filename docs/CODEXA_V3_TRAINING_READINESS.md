## Safe future continuation

`codexa continuation prepare` is a separately callable conversion/dry-verification operation. It has **no training action**. It checks the original checkpoint SHA256, native v1 schema, exact architecture, tied finite weights, frozen tokenizer and an immutable audited dataset manifest. Changed data requires a recorded exclusion proof for the parent dataset. Existing exact-resume configuration/data fingerprints remain strict; no legacy resume check is weakened.

The default explicit policy preserves AdamW moments and all optimizer update counters, while starting a fresh warmup/cosine schedule. Incomplete/nonfinite/mismapped moments, negative second moments or incompatible counters cause failure. `--optimizer-policy reset` creates a separately named fresh bundle and records the reset; it is not described as exact optimizer continuation. Parent RNG state is retained. An unchanged dataset preserves sampler order; a proven-disjoint new dataset receives a fresh Feistel order. Historical run directories are never overwritten.

Optimizer-origin metadata separates lifetime model updates from steps since an explicit optimizer reset. Synthetic non-training tests cover later preservation after a reset, as well as rejected negative moments and mismatched counters. An actual-checkpoint in-memory conversion preserved all 170 optimizer states and every model tensor, retained 500,006,912 cumulative tokens at phase step zero, and made zero updates. ## Acceptance gates before conversational fine-tuning

Keep the diagnostic report's gates; do not lower them to match current results:

- At least 90% grammatical, sensible completions on ≥100 independent sentence starts; ≥90% grammar discrimination on a sufficiently diverse independent set.
- At least 80% coherent 128–256-token paragraphs on ≥100 independent contexts, preserving topic, entities and logical progression. Use two blinded reviewers on a subset and record disagreements.
- Severe loops in no more than 5% of sampled paragraphs across the fixed three seeds; disclose greedy failures and repetition distributions too.
- At least 80% elementary factual correctness on ≥100 clear associations, assessed by both candidate scoring and verified free generation. Reject contradictions and ambiguous scoring shortcuts.
- Stable full old/new held-out and source-specific losses; predeclare a nonregression tolerance, such as 0.05 nats, while requiring generation improvement.
- Exact tokenizer reconstruction, no unintended UNK/PAD, finite compatible states, intact fingerprints and reliable recovery. Context preservation needs a more diverse panel than the current single template.

Current evidence fails the paragraph, repetition, factual and contextual-generation gates. A chat template or instruction tuning alone is not demonstrated to remedy these failures.
